# Hop Shot
A micro dexterity game for 2 players in 10 minutes inspired by the Japanese sport of pole toppling.

## Components
* 4 discs: 2 each of black and natural
* 1 pole
* 1 reference card

## Setup
1. Select a play surface; ideally a smooth table or play mat around 2-3' per side
2. Each player selects a color and places their discs along the edge of the play surface closest to themselves
3. Set the pole and card aside
4. Players take turns flicking one disc into play
5. Place the pole on top of the final disc to be flicked

## Gameplay
Players take turns flicking their discs attempting to knock over the pole while airborn, twice.

### Turns
The current player flicks both of their discs in either order with the following results:
* Discs that leave play are returned to the edge of the play surface
    * If any are opponent's discs do not flick your other disc
* If pole is knocked over:
    * If ramping see Ramp section below
    * Otherwise put pole back and do not flick your other disc

The player ends their turn by placing the pole on their least recently flicked disc.

### Ramp
There must be a disc between the pole and one of the current player's discs to attempt to ramp:
1. Hold the card against the disc making a ramp and aiming it at the pole
2. Flick the player's disc up the card toward the pole

If the disc hits the pole while still airborne and the pole falls over the ramp is successful.  
Either way do not flick your other disc.

## Game End
The first player to successfully ramp twice wins.
