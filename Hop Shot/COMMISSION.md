# Requirements

The goal is a web client via Godot for performing final playtesting and demos as essentially a physics sandbox. There will be no game logic or artwork; only plain shapes, controls and networking.

Completed project due by August 31, 2026

## Components
- 4 discs (Crokinole style for reference)
- 1 pole
- 1 ramp
- 1 card
- 1 raised play surface with invisible boundary walls

The card component will need to adjust its properties depending on whether at rest or not so it can be set on a disc to serve as a ramp but while at rest be firm enough to not be knocked away when actually used as a ramp. IRL a player could use their free hand to hold the card still; perhaps using a keybind to simulate this would also work.

## Controls
- components: pick up (re-orient on pick up), move, drop, rotate, flick (angle / strength)
- camera: free movement

## Players
- 2 active seats that can be joined / left allowing control of componets
- observers with camera controls though no control of components (ideally unlimited but at least 4)

# Observed Challenges

Other virtual tabletop solutions fail primarily with interaction between discs and ramp: when a disc is flicked toward a ramp they collide with the ramp edge being deflected or otherwise affected rather than proceeding smoothly up the ramp.

The idea is that a player flicks a disc so that it travels across the play surface, up the ramp and then goes airborne with goal of knocking over the pole while the disc is still in the air; being able to control flick strength will be important.
