# Leap Frog
A micro dexterity game for 2 players in 10 minutes inspired by the Japanese sport of pole toppling.

## Components
* 4 frog discs: 2 each of black and natural
* 1 fly pole
* 1 ramp

## Setup
1. Select a play surface; ideally a smooth table or play mat around 2-3' per side
2. Each player selects a color and places their discs along the edge of the play surface closest to themselves
3. Set the fly and ramp aside
4. Players take turns flicking one frog into play
5. Place the fly on top of the final frog to be flicked; this frog is now focused

## Gameplay
Players take turns flicking their frogs attempting to feast on the fly twice.

### Turns
The current player flicks both of their frogs with the goal of feasting on the fly with the following results:
* Flicking your frog out of play leaves it frazzled; frazzled frogs cannot be flicked for one turn
* Opponent frogs become frantic when flung from play; frantic frogs are flicked twice for one turn
* Focused frogs become frazzled when bumped such that the fly falls over; this ends the player's turn
* Frogs may attempt to feast (see below); this ends the player's turn

The player's last flicked frog frightens the fly toward their other frog which becomes focused.

### Feast
Should there be a frog between the fly and one of the current player's frogs they may attempt to feast:
1. Place the ramp against the central frog
2. Flick the player's frog up the ramp toward the fly
3. If the frog hits the fly and the fly falls over the feast is successful

## Game End
The first player to successfully feast twice wins.
